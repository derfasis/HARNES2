# Commercial task fidelity: Stage 0 cases, version 1

`benchmarks/commercial-task-fidelity-v1/cases.json` contains four newly authored synthetic conversation fixtures. They exercise distinct evidence problems: a first-person paid-recommendation task with temporary constraints, an open comparison of independent monetization options with an explicit no-MLM preference, a vendor's quotation of another person's request beside a different author's capacity complaint, and an older task later closed by the same author in another thread.

These fixtures are test material only. They are not observations from a market, an audience sample, evidence of prevalence, or proof that anyone is a real lead. Their `source_ref` values explicitly identify synthetic sources. They were authored for this stage and do not reuse the earlier 500-message corpus or the previous fitness-source cases.

The intended checks concern attribution, task ownership, time-bounded conditions, reply ancestry, and the difference between historical interest and later status. Preserve the complete original message text and links between replies when testing. In particular, the current inventory and video constraints in the Russian case are conditional and temporary; the October 10 Ukrainian cancellation is newer current-status evidence but does not erase the October 1 historical task.

The cases do not establish whether any task fits the current FitLine offer. If required offer facts or compatibility evidence are absent, offer fit stays `UNKNOWN`. The fixture set makes no claim about provider novelty, unseen examples, comparative model performance, or commercial outcomes. It provides no authority to contact anyone or to treat an inferred task as permission to send a message.
