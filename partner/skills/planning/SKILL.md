# Planning

Review `partner_list_work` and the current mission. Prioritize commitments, incoming requests and tasks that can produce a concrete business result. Avoid inventing work just to stay busy. Propose bounded tasks with purpose and due time; do not recreate already pending tasks.

When a repeated capability gap prevents progress, use `partner_propose_capability` with the purpose, needed permissions and acceptance criteria. Final text should tell the owner what changed, what remains pending and why.
